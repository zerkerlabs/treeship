- **`@treeship/verify` verifies whole packages (W2-6, PKG-2).** `verifyPackage(files, { pinnedKeys })` checks every artifact envelope's Ed25519 signatures in core-wasm, binds each envelope to the receipt's `artifact_id` and full `digest`, and checks the signed close record against the exact bytes of `receipt.json`. It returns the CLI's verdicts: `verified` only under keys you pin, `signatures-pass` when a signer is known only from the package's `keys.json`, `structural-pass` for `receipt.json` alone (`scope: 'receipt-only'`) or for packages holding approvals, endorsements or rooms, whose rules it leaves to `treeship package verify` (`scope: 'partial'`), and `failed` otherwise. The T2 vectors gain `verify_js` and `verify_js_pinned` columns: every tampered package is `failed` or, for the two room vectors, capped at `structural-pass`; none reads `signatures-pass` or `verified`. `verifyReceipt` is unchanged and still structural only.
