- **A redacted trace verifies as a redacted trace.** Removing an artifact's envelope from a sealed package was indistinguishable from never having had it: the Merkle tree still commits to the id, so the verifier reported an envelope that should be there and was not. That is the right answer for tampering and the wrong one for sharing a trace with parts withheld. A package may now carry `redaction.json`, a statement the producer signs listing what it withheld and the parent each withheld step named. The verifier honours it only when the signature verifies under a key the package is already judged by and the statement names this package's session and Merkle root, so an unsigned marker cannot excuse a stripped step and a redaction cannot be lifted from one trace onto another. A withheld artifact reports as withheld rather than missing, the chain still walks, and `chain_linkage` warns that the edges across withheld steps are the producer's assertion rather than a signed link, because the link lives inside the envelope being withheld. Any withheld artifact caps the verdict at the new `verified-partial`: everything disclosed checks out and the reader is holding part of a trace, which the word now says.
