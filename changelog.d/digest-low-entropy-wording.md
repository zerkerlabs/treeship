- **A digest is a commitment, not redaction.** `TREESHIP.md` said that without the original arguments "the digest reveals nothing", and `wrap`'s output-summary comment said a digest "discloses nothing". Neither holds when the committed value is guessable: a digest over a small enumeration, a short identifier or a bounded amount is recovered by hashing the candidates. Both places now say what a digest actually gives you, and point at the enclosing object or the salted selective-disclosure path for low-entropy values. No behaviour changes.
