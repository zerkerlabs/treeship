- **`treeship package redact` writes a copy of a trace with chosen steps withheld.** Three things leave the copy for each withheld artifact: its signed envelope, its timeline entries, and its recorded process executions. The third matters most, because the envelope holds the argv and the digests while the receipt holds the command, so withholding the envelope alone would have hidden the proof and left the thing being proved in the clear. Entries are found by the `artifact_id` they now carry rather than by matching timestamps or names, since a redaction that silently misses an entry tells someone their data is gone when it is not. `preview.html` is re-rendered from the redacted receipt, because it embeds the receipt and a stale one would serve the withheld content straight back. The original close record is kept rather than re-signed: it still proves what was sealed at close, and the signed redaction names both receipt digests, so the derived view is provably the producer's own. The copy reads as `verified-partial`.
