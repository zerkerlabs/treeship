- **An unjoined invitation passes `package verify --strict`.** An honest host that minted an invitation nobody redeemed failed `chain_completeness` under `--strict`: the invitation is sealed but unchained, and only a redeemed invitation counted as bound. An invitation an authenticated signer issued for this very session (its signed `session_ref` names the session) is now bound by that signature, joined or not; the row says "invitation issued for this session".
