- **A receipt's own account of a session now points at the evidence for it.** Timeline entries and recorded process executions carried no link back to the artifact they were sealed as, so nothing in the narrative could be traced to the signed record behind it. That gap blocks sharing a trace with parts withheld: a producer withholding an artifact had no way to find the entries describing it, and the command it meant to withhold stayed in `side_effects.processes` in the clear. Matching by timestamp or name would have been a guess, and a redaction that silently misses an entry is worse than none. `TimelineEntry` and `ProcessExecution` now carry an optional `artifact_id`, `wrap` names the artifact it just sealed on its completion event, and the receipt composer propagates it. Absent on events that seal nothing, and on every receipt written before the field existed.
