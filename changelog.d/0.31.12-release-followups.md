- **Release lockfile job and hub deploy, follow-ups.** The `refresh-lockfiles` job updates an already-open lockfile PR on a re-run instead of failing on the existing branch, and has a 45-minute timeout. The hub Dockerfile prefers `RAILWAY_GIT_COMMIT_SHA` over a `HUB_COMMIT` left on the service, so a connected-repository deploy never reports a stale commit; `release.sh prepare` no longer calls the lockfile refresh a manual step.
