- **The session receipt carries what the judges said.** A session's `judgement.v1` receipts were sealed into the package and read by `package verify`, but nothing about them reached `receipt.json`, so every consumer holding the receipt without the envelopes, the report, the pull-request comment and anything reading the JSON, was blind to them. `session close` now fills a `judgements` section with the total, the distinct judges by the same name the verifier reports, and separate counts for refused, escalated and resolved. The counts are kept apart deliberately: a refusal, an open escalation and a closed one mean different things to a reviewer, and one number lets the worst of them hide in the total. A session nobody judged has no section at all rather than a band of zeroes, because silence and a clean bill are not the same claim. The section records what the caller's own record says and never re-runs a judge; the package verifier still derives the same facts independently from the envelopes, which are what is signed.
